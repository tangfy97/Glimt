# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Glimt is an image viewer and sorter for macOS, made by TangSpark. This policy covers every version of Glimt: the Mac App Store version and the one on GitHub.

**Glimt collects no data.** It never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or update checks, and no third-party code.

What Glimt does on your Mac:

- It reads the folders you open, to show the pictures and videos in them.
- It changes files only when you ask: moving, copying, renaming, rotating, tagging, or moving them to the Trash.
- It keeps small thumbnails of pictures you have viewed in its cache folder (`~/Library/Caches`, or the app's container in the App Store version), so folders open faster next time. You can clear it in Settings.
- It remembers your settings, your sort folders, recently opened folders, and, in the App Store version, the folders you have allowed it to open. These stay on your Mac.

None of this leaves your Mac. Links in the Help menu open in your web browser, which is outside Glimt.
